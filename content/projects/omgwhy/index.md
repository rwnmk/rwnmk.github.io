## What this is

A completely serious scientific instrument for post-game analysis in Apex. After a loss, the name of whoever is responsible is recorded here. There have only been two observed causes:

- **Hubris**: "I can take this fight lol"
- **Positioning**: "I can stand here lol"

## Data

Everything is saved in localStorage, so it's all stored per device, clearing your site data clears the evidence (but not your name).
